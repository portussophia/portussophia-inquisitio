
# PortusInquisitio™


**A Haven of Inquiry**


PortusInquisitio™ is situated under the PortusSophia™ research programme as a developing context for disciplined inquiry, scholarly examination, and the preservation of distinctions.


Its orientation is toward inquiry without coercive capture, compelled correspondence, or premature closure.


## Name and Formation


The designation **PortusInquisitio** brings two Latin nouns into relation:


- **Portus** — harbor, haven, passage, or refuge.

- **Inquisitio** — searching into, investigation, examination, or inquiry.


Their conjunction suggests *a haven of inquiry*: a place where examination may proceed rigorously without requiring a predetermined conclusion.


This is a proposed relational interpretation of the compound, not a claim that the complete designation is historically attested in Classical Latin.

## Inquiry Posture


PortusInquisitio™ maintains the distinction between:


- Source and interpretation

- Attestation and creative construction

- Observation and inference

- Correspondence and imposed equivalence

- Preservation and adoption

- Scholarly contribution and institutional authority


An inquiry may disclose correspondence, difference, uncertainty, or no correspondence found.


None of these outcomes requires forced resolution.
