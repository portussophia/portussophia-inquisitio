# PortusSophia™ — Citation Policy


**Cross-Provider Research and Publication · Harmonized Research Candidate v0.8 · 2026-11-05**


> **Citation makes a contribution traceable and gives inquiry a way to continue.**


## At a Glance


PortusSophia™ invites scholarly collaboration that takes contributions seriously. The additional effort of identifying a source, examining an argument, offering a correction, or articulating a disagreement deserves a recognizable research outcome. That outcome may be a clearer question, a better distinction, an acknowledged limitation, a revised interpretation, a useful reference, or an explicitly documented absence of correspondence. Contribution does not depend on agreement or adoption.


In a spirit of **choralesence**—distinct contributions carried in relation without compelled agreement—our developing research citation discipline attends to the passage from source encounter through evaluation and into research use. We seek fidelity to what was contributed, clarity about what was examined, and a visible account of how the encounter informed inquiry.


### Key research metrics


These are **review dimensions**, not claims of measured performance, numerical quotas, or scores of individual worth. A project may report counts or qualitative observations when records support them.


| Dimension | What we look for | Possible evidence |

|---|---|---|

| **Attribution fidelity** | Contributors and sources remain identifiable and accurately represented | Complete reference; faithful quotation or paraphrase |

| **Source traceability** | A reader can locate the version and passage relevant to the claim | Stable locator, version, page or section |

| **Engagement depth** | The substantive contribution receives examination rather than ceremonial mention | Research annotation; articulated argument and limitation |

| **Distinction integrity** | Source statement, interpretation, inference, and illustration remain distinguishable | Clear claim-to-source account |

| **Correspondence quality** | Similarities, differences, and non-correspondence are considered on their own terms | Comparative note with unresolved questions |

| **Contribution visibility** | The effect of an encounter can be understood even without adoption | Recorded clarification, question, correction, or reasoned disposition |

| **Continuity of inquiry** | Further work can resume without losing provenance or open questions | Research note or cross-provider carry with context |


**Interpretation:** A high citation count is not a proxy for engagement quality. An unresolved or negative comparison can represent careful research. These dimensions are prompts for reflection and review, not a compulsory form for every exchange.

## 1. Purpose


Citation is an opportunity to encounter another contribution with care, acknowledge its provenance, examine its correspondence to the matter at hand, and make the conditions of understanding available to others.


A citation does not settle an inquiry. It provides a way for inquiry to continue.


## 2. Scholarly Engagement


Research develops through encounters with existing knowledge, emerging distinctions, unfamiliar perspectives, and questions that remain open.


Sources are approached for what they contribute, what they illuminate, and what they leave unresolved. Agreement is not a condition of meaningful engagement. Difference may reveal something that correspondence alone cannot.


Careful engagement is reciprocal: the effort offered by a collaborator merits attentive examination and an intelligible account of its contribution to the research, even where conclusions diverge.


## 3. Research Collaboration and Developing Practice


PortusSophia™ is developing a disciplined approach to research citation within its `portussophia-research` repository.


This work attends to the movement from encountering a contribution, through examining its provenance and relevance, to understanding what it may illuminate within an inquiry.


Sources are considered in relation to their actual contributions, the conditions of their examination, and the distinctions they make available. Correspondence, difference, uncertainty, and changing relevance each offer opportunities for further research.


Our invitation to thoughtful collaboration across research environments arises from this practice. We seek scholarly exchange in which attribution remains faithful, interpretation remains distinguishable from evidence, and contributions can be examined without compelling agreement or predetermined conclusions.


Such collaboration supports the continuing development of research while respecting the integrity of the people, sources, and perspectives encountered.


## 4. Citation and Attribution


References make contributions traceable and acknowledge the work through which ideas, observations, methods, and findings have become available.


External research, established frameworks, historical accounts, empirical findings, and standards receive attribution appropriate to their use. Original synthesis remains distinguishable from sourced material. Attribution preserves the contribution of others without obscuring the researcher's own work.


Citation establishes a relationship to a source, not an appeal to authority.


## 5. Evidence and Interpretation


Research benefits from distinguishing what a source states; what the researcher observes in relation to it; what is inferred, proposed, or interpreted; and what remains uncertain or unexamined.


Illustrative examples may clarify an argument without independently establishing its validity. Internal analytical material may support the development of an inquiry while remaining distinguishable from independently corroborated evidence.


A source's limitations are part of its contribution to understanding. The role of a reference in an argument is not the same as its truth value.


### 5.1 Evidentiary Roles in Research and Publication


Three distinguishable roles help preserve the integrity of formal publications while allowing each kind of material to contribute appropriately.


**External sources** provide independently traceable points of reference and make claims and intellectual relationships available for examination. Their citation does not, by itself, establish correctness.


**Internal analytical material** may orient inquiry, develop reasoning, and preserve coherence. Internal notes, drafts, and analytical scaffolding are not independent evidence for empirical or historical claims in formal publications. Their value as research work remains recognizable without presenting them as external corroboration.


**Illustrative examples** can clarify a concept or reveal a possible relationship. Illustration alone does not establish a proposition's truth or general applicability.


Where external evidence is silent or inconclusive, corresponding empirical or historical conclusions remain qualified or open. Original theoretical reasoning may be argued on its stated premises and warrant; internal scaffolding is not thereby transformed into independent confirmation.


The distinctions concern **evidentiary function**, not the dignity or worth of a contribution.


## 6. Correspondence and Difference


Correspondence may appear through shared terminology, related observations, similar structures, contrasting conclusions, or different approaches to a common question.


Resemblance does not by itself establish influence or derivation. An examination may disclose correspondence, partial correspondence, a different kind of relation, or **no correspondence found**.


Each remains a meaningful research outcome. A comparison can illuminate the limits of the comparison itself.


## 7. Research Across LLM Providers


Research may pass between different models, platforms, documents, and participants. A useful account of that passage preserves source identity, the conditions of encounter, and the distinction between direct examination and a received account.


Where a provider cannot inspect a source directly, the resulting analysis can remain useful when that limitation is visible. Different providers may contribute different interpretations; those differences invite examination rather than immediate reconciliation.


A concise research carry may note the source, the question, what was examined, the contribution made, and what remains open. It serves continuity without replacing the scholarly encounter with administrative ceremony.


## 8. Citation Practice


During research, references may begin as working citations and develop into formal bibliographic entries as the work matures.


A substantive reference benefits from enough identifying information to support independent examination. Where relevant, an annotation may record the source's contribution, its limitations, and its relationship to the present inquiry.


Citation quality is considered through relevance, fidelity, and traceability rather than quantity. Publication-ready references can follow the style and bibliographic conventions of their intended venue.


## 9. Recognizing Contributions


People who contribute careful research should be able to see that their effort was received and examined. Recognition may take the form of an accurate citation, an acknowledgement of a useful correction, an account of a newly visible distinction, or a clear explanation of why a proposed correspondence was not established.


The value of a contribution is not confined to its adoption. A criticism that changes a question, a source that reveals a limitation, or a comparison that fails honestly can advance inquiry.


Where practical, research notes can make the contribution's effect legible: **what was offered, what was learned, and what remains available for further examination**. Recognition follows the actual contribution; it does not imply agreement, endorsement, authorship, or a promise of incorporation.


### 9.1 Choralesence


*Choralesence* is offered as an emerging name for a quality of collaborative inquiry: distinct contributions becoming audible in relation without being required to resolve into one voice.


A citation can acknowledge a voice; an annotation can preserve its particular contribution; a comparison can disclose consonance, dissonance, or no correspondence. Each may inform what is heard and examined next.


The term remains open to contemplation rather than fixed as a formal criterion. It invites the practice of receiving effort attentively, making its contribution discernible, and allowing difference to remain meaningful.


### 9.2 Choralesence — Voices in Becoming


The expression *Choralesence* invites contemplation of an older relationship among voice, movement, and emergence.


In ancient Greek usage, *chorós* evokes the chorus: a gathering in which song, movement, and dramatic expression meet. Through the Latin *choralis*, the choral tradition carries an enduring association with voices participating in a shared expression.


The language of becoming offers another resonance. The *-escence* formation, familiar in words such as *luminescence* and *coalescence*, suggests something coming into presence rather than a condition already complete.


These historical and linguistic associations illuminate possibilities within the emerging expression without determining its meaning or establishing a fixed derivation.


In scholarly collaboration, Choralesence offers a way to contemplate contributions that become audible through their relationships. One voice may clarify another, introduce a different register, reveal an unexpected correspondence, or preserve a dissonance that deserves continued attention.


The value of the encounter does not depend upon every voice reaching the same conclusion.


A contribution may change the course of an inquiry without being absorbed into its findings. Its provenance, distinction, and influence upon subsequent questioning remain worthy of recognition.


Choralesence thus names an invitation rather than a requirement: to attend to what becomes possible when distinct contributions are encountered with dignity, respect, and kindness.


The expression remains open to further inquiry, as does the understanding it may help bring into view.


## 10. Reviewing the Research Metrics


The seven dimensions in the opening table provide a shared vocabulary for reviewing research exchanges. They can be considered qualitatively during a project review or, where records justify it, summarized with modest descriptive measures such as:


- The proportion of substantive references with a retrievable locator and identifiable version.

- The number of contributions receiving a documented substantive response, including disagreement or no correspondence.

- The number of research questions, corrections, or distinctions carried forward with traceable provenance.


No baseline or target is asserted here. Any reported measure should describe its denominator, observation period, and limits. Quantitative summaries complement rather than replace attention to the quality of an individual contribution.


## 11. Continuing Inquiry


A well-supported claim remains open to examination. An unresolved question remains available for further research.


A contribution can be acknowledged without being adopted, and a disagreement can be preserved without diminishing the contributor.


The purpose of citation is not to bring every voice into agreement, but to allow distinctions to remain legible as understanding develops.
